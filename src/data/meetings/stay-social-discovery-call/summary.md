### Current System Overview

- Stay Social provides events, classes, and clubs for adults with intellectual disabilities and neurodiversity
- Two funding tracks exist through regional centers:
  1. Self-determination path (clients create their own budget)
  2. Social recreation path (traditional track with 6-month funding periods)
- Current client management involves manual processes across multiple platforms:
  - QuickBooks for client data, notes, and invoicing
  - Eventbrite for event registration using promo codes
  - Email for communication with clients/caregivers
  - Waiver Forever for waivers (recently switched for HIPAA compliance)

### Key Challenges

- Client data scattered across tools; no single client 360 or clear funding status
- Manual creation and management of promo codes in Eventbrite
- Time‑consuming invoice matching across FMS emails and QuickBooks
- Limited ability to quickly find client information and funding status
- Time-consuming process to send notifications about events
- Manual onboarding process for new clients and unclear intake data destination

### Proposed Direction

- Central dashboard with a unified balance ledger per client instead of multiple codes
- Automated confirmations, reminders, and changes via email and SMS
- Integrations with QuickBooks and Eventbrite using OAuth, backfill, and webhook‑driven sync
- Streamlined client onboarding process Intake form that populates client profiles and downstream systems

### Implementation Options

- Build custom solution from scratch
- Use existing platforms/CRMs with automation tools
- Consider cost-effectiveness of each approach based on business size and needs
