### Current Registration Issues

- Google Form creates confusion when people think they're registered after filling it out

- Spreadsheet management described as a "nightmare" and "very sloppy"
- Information scattered across multiple systems (Google Docs, Excel, email)
- Hours of time spent on manual administration

### Desired Portal Solution

- Single portal where participants can:
  - Fill out the interest form
  - Have service coordinator complete screening tool (via link)
  - Register for events
  - RSVP for specific workshop dates
- Portal should integrate with organizer's website
- Need option for "hidden" or private events

### Technical Requirements

- Pull client data from:
  - Eventbrite (per event, per client) - "CLIENT MANAGEMENT"
  - QuickBooks (all funding information) - "FUNDING"
- Different authentication levels:
  - Admin (full access)
  - ARC (limited access to ARC-specific information)
  - Client (portal access)
- Allow staff to check event attendance and see important information (allergies, photo waivers)
- Ability to take notes for each client per event

### Spencer's Proposed Solution

- CRM system with searchable client profiles
- All client information accessible in one place
- Events management with attendee tracking
- Ability to pull data from Eventbrite and QuickBooks
